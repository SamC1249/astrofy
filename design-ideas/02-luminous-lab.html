01 / About

I'm a sophomore at Harvard from Boston, studying Computer Science, Mathematics, and Psychology. My research lives where those three meet: I work on mechanistic interpretability in the Computation, Cognition, and Development Lab and on hierarchical reinforcement learning under Kempner and the Brantley Lab.

Before research pulled me in, debate did — I peaked 6th nationally in Public Forum, then founded Outreach Debate to open the activity up: 1,800+ members and 300+ volunteers later, it's still running. Along the way I built two education businesses doing 5–6 figures in revenue.

Field note: the same instinct drives all of it — take a black box (a transformer, a resolution, a market) and pry it open until the mechanism is legible.
