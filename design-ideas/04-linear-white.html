About

My research sits at the intersection of how machines learn and how people think — pulling apart transformer internals on one side, and building agents that plan over long horizons on the other.

Before research, I spent years in competitive debate, peaking 6th nationally in Public Forum. That turned into Outreach Debate, a nonprofit I founded that grew to 1,800+ members and 300+ volunteers, and two education businesses that reached five to six figures in revenue.

Most recently, I did engineering at AGI House, building people-search infrastructure.
