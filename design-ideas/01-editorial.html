§ i  Abstract

Sam Chen is a sophomore at Harvard from Boston, studying computer science, mathematics, and psychology. His research sits at the seam between machine and mind: mechanistic interpretability in the Computation, Cognition, and Development Lab,1 and hierarchical reinforcement learning under the Kempner Institute and the Brantley Lab.2

Previously, he built people-search infrastructure as an engineer at AGI House.3 Before that, a decade-adjacent career in competitive debate — peaking 6th nationally in Public Forum — which he converted into Outreach Debate, a free coaching organization now counting 1,800+ members and 300+ volunteers, alongside two education businesses doing five-to-six-figure revenue.

The through-line, if one must be named: taking opaque systems — neural networks, admissions, argument — and making them legible to people standing outside them.
